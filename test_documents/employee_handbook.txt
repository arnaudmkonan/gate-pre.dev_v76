ACME Corporation Employee Handbook
===================================

Welcome to ACME Corporation!

This handbook provides essential information about company policies, 
benefits, and expectations for all employees.

CHAPTER 1: COMPANY VALUES
-------------------------

At ACME Corporation, we believe in:
- Innovation: Pushing boundaries to create better solutions
- Integrity: Acting with honesty in all our dealings  
- Teamwork: Collaborating to achieve common goals
- Excellence: Striving for the highest quality in everything we do

CHAPTER 2: EMPLOYMENT POLICIES
------------------------------

Working Hours:
Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
Flexible work arrangements may be available with manager approval.

Dress Code:
Business casual attire is expected. Client-facing roles may require
formal business attire on meeting days.

Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week after completing
their 90-day probationary period.

CHAPTER 3: BENEFITS
-------------------

Health Insurance:
Full-time employees are eligible for medical, dental, and vision
coverage starting on their first day. The company covers 80% of
premium costs for employees and 50% for dependents.

Retirement:
401(k) plan with company match up to 4% of salary.
Fully vested after 3 years of employment.

Paid Time Off:
- 15 days PTO for employees with 0-2 years of service
- 20 days PTO for employees with 3-5 years of service
- 25 days PTO for employees with 6+ years of service

Holidays:
The company observes 10 paid holidays per year, including:
New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day,
Thanksgiving (2 days), Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and 
2 floating holidays.

CHAPTER 4: PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
-----------------------------------

Training Budget:
Each employee receives $2,500 annually for professional development,
including courses, certifications, and conference attendance.

Mentorship Program:
New employees are paired with experienced mentors to help them
integrate into the company culture and advance their careers.

CHAPTER 5: WORKPLACE CONDUCT
----------------------------

Harassment Policy:
ACME Corporation has zero tolerance for harassment of any kind.
All employees must complete annual harassment prevention training.

Confidentiality:
Employees must protect confidential company and client information.
Non-disclosure agreements must be signed upon hiring.

Contact Information:
Human Resources: hr@acmecorp.com
IT Support: helpdesk@acmecorp.com
Emergency: security@acmecorp.com
